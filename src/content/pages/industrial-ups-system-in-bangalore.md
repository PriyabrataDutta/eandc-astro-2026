## Reliable Industrial UPS System for Critical Power Requirements

Industrial operations need more than basic battery backup. Fluctuations, voltage disturbances and unexpected power failures can interfere with equipment and interrupt ongoing processes.

An industrial UPS system helps provide a continuous and controlled power supply to connected loads. Online UPS technology is particularly suitable for critical applications because the load receives conditioned power while the system provides backup during an interruption.

E&C manufactures Online UPS systems for a wide range of applications, supporting sectors such as manufacturing, healthcare, banking and financial services, government organisations and corporate facilities.
