## What E&C works towards, and how we assure quality

Our purpose is to deliver dependable power solutions with customer-first service and a strong commitment to quality standards.

## Our Vision

To be the most preferred power solution provider in India.

## Our Mission

Achieve customer delight through quality power solution products and committed services.

## Our Quality Policy

**Electronics & Controls Power Systems Pvt Ltd** is committed to deliver quality and environment-friendly products and services that satisfy customers, and accomplish leadership through:

-   Defined measurable quality and environmental objectives
-   Compliance with legal and statutory requirements
-   Pollution prevention approach
-   Utilization of adequate resources
-   Participation by stakeholders
-   Compliance with quality and environmental management systems
-   Regular reviews for continual improvement
