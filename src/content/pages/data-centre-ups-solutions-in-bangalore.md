## How to Select the Right UPS System for Your Data Centre

Choosing a UPS system requires a detailed understanding of the facility's electrical requirements and operational priorities. An appropriately selected system should provide sufficient power capacity, the required backup duration and compatibility with the existing electrical infrastructure.

E&C Power helps businesses consider the essential factors involved in selecting suitable data centre power protection equipment.

### Evaluate the Total IT Load

The first step is to determine the total electrical load of the equipment that requires UPS protection.

This includes servers, storage devices, network switches, routers and other essential IT equipment. The assessment should also account for expected load variations and planned infrastructure expansion.

Accurate load estimation helps determine the required UPS capacity and reduces the risk of selecting an unsuitable system.

### Determine the Required Backup Duration

Backup requirements vary depending on the facility's operational needs and emergency power arrangements.

Some data centres require sufficient battery autonomy to maintain operations until a generator becomes available, while others need additional backup time to complete essential processes or perform a controlled shutdown.

The UPS and battery system should be configured according to the required runtime at the expected operating load.

### Consider Redundancy and System Availability

Data centres supporting critical applications may require additional power protection to reduce the risk of a single equipment failure interrupting operations.

Depending on the facility's requirements, redundant UPS arrangements, parallel configurations and alternative power distribution paths may be considered.

The appropriate configuration should be determined through an assessment of the facility's availability objectives, electrical architecture and maintenance requirements.

### Plan for Maintenance and Long-Term Reliability

Routine inspection, battery health monitoring and preventive maintenance are important for maintaining dependable UPS performance.

Data centre operators should consider equipment accessibility, maintenance requirements, replacement battery availability and the overall lifecycle cost when selecting a power backup system.

A well-planned maintenance programme helps identify potential problems before they affect critical IT operations.
