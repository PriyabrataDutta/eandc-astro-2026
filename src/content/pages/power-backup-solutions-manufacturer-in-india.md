Comprehensive Power Backup Solutions for Different Requirements

Every organisation has different power protection requirements. A small office may require a compact UPS, while a manufacturing facility, healthcare institution or data centre may need a high-capacity Online UPS with reliable battery backup.

E&C offers multiple categories of power products, including Online UPS, Line Interactive UPS, domestic and industrial inverters, solar solutions, batteries, stabilizers, industrial battery chargers, remote monitoring solutions and other power accessories.

Online UPS Systems

E&C manufactures Online UPS systems across a broad capacity range. Its product portfolio includes Accenta, Renata-I, Renata-300, Renata-DS, Renata Modular, Accenta Rack and Lithium UPS systems.

These solutions are designed for applications where continuous and clean power is important, including IT infrastructure, healthcare, banking, manufacturing and other critical environments.

Industrial Power Backup Solutions

Industrial environments often require power systems capable of supporting demanding equipment and continuous operations. E&C provides Industrial Online UPS and Industrial Inverter solutions for applications where reliable power protection is essential.

Its industrial UPS portfolio includes specialised systems such as MIL Grade UPS and IP 41–55 Online UPS solutions.

Line Interactive UPS

For applications with comparatively less critical power requirements, E&C offers Line Interactive UPS systems. Its Cheerups range covers 600 VA to 5 kVA and includes features such as AVR-based voltage stabilisation, battery-mode efficiency and protection against power disturbances.

Batteries and Battery Charging Solutions

A dependable backup system also depends on the right battery technology. E&C's product range includes SMF, tubular, Ni-Cd, GEL and lithium-ion batteries. The company also manufactures industrial battery chargers for different power applications.
