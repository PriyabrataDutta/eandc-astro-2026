## Sales and support across India

E&C products are available across India through our dedicated regional sales centers, backed by experienced teams for solution guidance and deployment support.

E&C's products can be bought by customers anywhere in India. We operate 8 sales centres across the country to meet your requirements with speed and reliability.

All our sales locations are equipped to provide the best power solutions based on your specific needs. Our experienced Sales Managers review your application details and recommend the most suitable solution.

E&C specializes in power continuity for data centers, mission-critical applications, media, operation theatres, medical equipment, CNC machinery, ATMs, continuous process industries, power plants, SCADA equipment, OB vans, VSAT communication equipment, and more. We also build custom products for Defence, Telecom, and Space applications under stringent specifications.
