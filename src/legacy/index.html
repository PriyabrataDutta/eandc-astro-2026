Applications

E&C is a major player in the Power Electronics industry in India serving a wide range of customers including Banks & Financial services, Government, Manufacturing, Retail and Corporate Customers.

Banking & Finance

Automated Banking needs uninterrupted Power and our UPS is being used for Servers and Desktops, Networks at Head Office, Branches and ATMs to provide continuous services while protecting the infrastructure from any electrical power problems.Non Banking Finance Companies and Share Broking firms use our UPS for share broking applications and for Customer services.

IT / ITES

Several Systems Integration Providers use our UPS for automation projects for a wide variety of applications - Rack Systems for captive power for Servers, Smart Racks, Clean Power for large mid to large IT Companies, Data Centres, etc.

Manufacturing

Manufacturing uses very sophisticated CNC Machines and precision tools to make parts and assemblies. Power outage leads to loss of job, expensive tools and time. Our UPS provides uninterrupted clean power helping industries deliver as per schedule. Precision and quality.

Health Care

Hospitals run facilities ranging from Operation Theatres, Diagnostics, In-Patient Care and a host of support services. Uninterrupted power is a basic requirement and our UPS are installed for Operation Theatres, Scanning equipments and general lighting backup purposes of many major hospitals.

Real Estate

Modern Real Estate firms employ many safety measures for fire fighting, emergency lighting and safe passenger elevators. All are connected to a Building Monitoring System which is critical and needs 24 x 7 power. Our UPS is used to provide smooth movement of passenger elevators, Emergency power for corridors and general backup lighting purposes. Large upscale residential properties use our UPS for 24 x7 power. Our Solar Systems are also used to harness solar energy during day time.

Defence

Defence uses Radar and other telemetry to protect the nation and these sophisticated instruments need clean and uninterrupted power. Our UPS are supplied to the Army, Airforce and Navy, and BSF and they are capable of shock and vibration resistant capable of mounting onboard Trucks and Ships.
