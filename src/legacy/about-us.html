Power Electronics Manufacturer in India

Popularly known as "E&C", We are one of India's most reputed manufacturers of Power solution products. With over three decades of experience in the field of Power electronics, E&C specializes in manufacturing Online UPS systems. With an extensive customer base across India, E&C is a well known brand enjoying a customer satisfaction rating of over 94%*.

Founded by Rtn. K. Ramachandra Iyer (popularly known as "Iyer"), a technocrat, in 1978, as Electronics & Controls, it was later converted into a Private Limited Company in 1997.

E&C's own customer support services are available in 10 regional offices and 48 dedicated service locations operating 24x7 across the country.

E&C is a major player in the Power Electronics industry in India serving a wide range of customers including Banks, Financial Services, Insurance, Government, Manufacturing, Retail and Corporate Customers

An ISO 9001 & 14001 accredited company, E&C follows very high quality standards for all its power products combining this with a strong Research and Development team. E&C's products are CE certified and have been tested by ETDC, SAMEER and ERTL for industry set parameters as well as environmental and safety standards. E&C's online UPS are certified by Bureau of Indian Standards(BIS).
