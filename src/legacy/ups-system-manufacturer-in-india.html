UPS System Manufacturer in India

When uninterrupted power is essential, choosing the right UPS system manufacturer can make a significant difference to the reliability of your operations. Electronics & Controls (E&C) is an established Indian manufacturer of Online UPS systems with more than 40 years of experience in power electronics. The company designs and manufactures UPS solutions using modern technology and is supported by dedicated teams across research and development, manufacturing, sales and service.

E&C provides UPS systems for different power requirements and applications, serving sectors such as banking and finance, IT and IT-enabled services, manufacturing, healthcare, real estate, government and defence. Its product portfolio includes multiple Online UPS ranges designed for different capacities and operating environments.

Reliable UPS Systems for Critical Power Requirements

A UPS system does more than provide backup during a power failure. For critical equipment, it helps maintain a dependable power supply and protects operations from interruptions that can affect productivity, equipment and essential services.

As a UPS system manufacturer in India, E&C offers a range of Online UPS systems covering capacities from smaller installations to large-scale power requirements. Its Online UPS portfolio includes Accenta, Renata-I, Renata-300, Renata-DS, Renata Modular, Accenta Rack and Lithium UPS models.

Online UPS Systems for Different Applications

E&C manufactures Online UPS systems for applications where continuous and stable power is important. Depending on the installation and capacity requirement, businesses can select from different UPS configurations and product ranges.

E&C's website specifically highlights the use of its UPS systems in servers, networks, ATMs, CNC machines, hospital equipment, building monitoring systems and other critical infrastructure.

UPS Manufacturing Expertise Backed by Engineering Experience

E&C's experience in power electronics extends back more than four decades. The company was founded in 1978 and later incorporated as Electronics & Controls Power Systems Private Limited in 1997. Its manufacturing and engineering capabilities are supported by dedicated R&D, manufacturing, sales and service teams.

This combination of engineering experience and manufacturing capability allows E&C to develop UPS systems for a variety of commercial, industrial and mission-critical requirements. The company also offers custom power solutions for applications requiring specific power protection configurations.

UPS Systems for Industries Across India

Different industries have different power continuity requirements. E&C manufactures UPS systems for a broad range of applications across India.

UPS for Banking and Finance

Banks, ATMs, financial institutions and share-broking operations depend on continuous availability of their IT and networking infrastructure. E&C UPS systems are used for servers, desktops, networks and ATM applications to support uninterrupted services.

UPS for IT and Data Centres

IT infrastructure requires dependable power for servers, networking equipment and other critical systems. E&C provides UPS solutions for rack systems, data centres and IT environments where power interruptions can affect business operations.

UPS for Manufacturing

Modern manufacturing facilities use CNC machines, precision equipment and automated systems that require stable power. An unexpected power interruption can interrupt production and potentially affect equipment and work in progress. E&C UPS systems are designed for such critical manufacturing applications.

UPS for Healthcare Facilities

Hospitals depend on continuous power for operating theatres, diagnostic equipment, patient-care infrastructure and other essential systems. E&C UPS solutions are used for applications including operation theatres, scanning equipment and general lighting backup.

E&C Power – UPS Manufacturing in Bengaluru

E&C's registered office and manufacturing operations are located in Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru, Karnataka, one of Bengaluru's established industrial zones. Peenya has a strong manufacturing ecosystem and convenient connectivity to different parts of the city, making the area an important industrial base for engineering and power-electronics businesses. E&C operates from its facility at 29/A, 2nd Phase, Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru.

Quality and Service Support

For a UPS system, product selection is only one part of the overall requirement. Installation, maintenance and service support are also important for long-term operation. E&C has its own customer support services through regional offices and dedicated service locations across India, supporting customers after installation.

The company states that it follows ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 quality standards and has a dedicated R&D team supporting its power-product development and manufacturing activities.

Choose the Right UPS System for Your Requirement

The appropriate UPS system depends on factors such as connected load, required capacity, application, operating environment, scalability and backup requirements. Businesses should evaluate these parameters before selecting a UPS manufacturer and system.

With a broad Online UPS portfolio, industrial power solutions and experience across sectors, E&C provides UPS systems for commercial, industrial and critical power applications across India. Its product range allows organisations to select a solution based on their specific power requirements rather than relying on a single UPS configuration.

For organisations looking for a UPS system manufacturer in India, E&C offers a combination of manufacturing expertise, product range, engineering capabilities and service support for diverse power protection requirements.
