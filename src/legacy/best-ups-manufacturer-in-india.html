Best UPS Manufacturer in India

Reliable power is essential when even a short interruption can affect business operations, equipment and critical services. As a trusted UPS manufacturer in India, Electronics & Controls Power Systems Pvt. Ltd. (E&C) develops and manufactures UPS systems for a wide range of commercial, industrial and mission-critical applications.

With more than 40 years of experience in power electronics, E&C has built its expertise around the design and manufacturing of Online UPS and other power protection systems. The company supports customers across sectors such as banking and finance, IT/ITES, manufacturing, healthcare, real estate and defence.

Reliable UPS Solutions Designed for Different Power Requirements

Every organisation has different power protection requirements. A small office may need a compact UPS, while a manufacturing facility, data centre or healthcare facility may require a higher-capacity industrial system.

E&C offers a broad range of UPS solutions to address these requirements. Its Online UPS portfolio includes the Accenta range from 1–10 kVA, Renata-I from 2–30 kVA, Renata-300 from 10–800 kVA, Renata-DS from 10–300 kVA, Renata Modular from 10–1200 kVA, Accenta Rack from 1–60 kVA and Lithium UPS from 1–5 kVA.

Online UPS Systems

Online UPS systems provide continuous power protection for applications where power quality and uninterrupted operation are important. E&C manufactures multiple Online UPS ranges designed for different capacity and application requirements.

These systems can be used across offices, IT infrastructure, banking environments, healthcare facilities, manufacturing units and other applications where dependable power is important.

Industrial Online UPS

Industrial environments often have demanding power requirements and sensitive equipment. E&C offers Industrial Online UPS solutions, including specialised systems such as MIL Grade UPS and IP-rated Online UPS options.

The company's MIL Grade UPS range, for example, is available from 1 kVA to 10 kVA and includes features such as true online double conversion, vibration and shock resistance, lithium battery compatibility and parallel redundancy.

Why Choose E&C as a UPS Manufacturer in India?

Choosing a UPS manufacturer involves more than simply selecting a power capacity. Product engineering, manufacturing expertise, application knowledge and after-sales support can all be important considerations.

Over Four Decades of Power Electronics Experience

E&C has been associated with power electronics since 1978 and has more than four decades of experience in the industry. Its operations include R&D, manufacturing, sales and service, supporting the development and delivery of power solutions for customers across India.

UPS Solutions Across Multiple Capacities

Different applications require different UPS capacities and configurations. E&C's product portfolio covers systems ranging from smaller 1 kVA solutions to high-capacity UPS systems reaching up to 1200 kVA in its Renata Modular range.

Applications Across Diverse Industries

E&C UPS systems are used across several sectors. According to the company's website, its applications include banking and finance, IT/ITES, manufacturing, healthcare, real estate and defence. These sectors use UPS systems for applications ranging from servers and networks to CNC machinery, medical equipment and critical infrastructure.

UPS Manufacturing and Power Solutions in Bengaluru

Bengaluru is one of India's major technology and industrial centres, with a diverse mix of IT companies, manufacturing facilities, healthcare organisations and commercial infrastructure. Peenya Industrial Area is an established industrial hub within the city, making Bengaluru a significant location for engineering and manufacturing businesses. E&C operates from its facility in the 2nd Phase of Peenya Industrial Area, where it provides its range of power-electronics products and services.

UPS Solutions for Critical Applications

Power interruptions can have different consequences depending on the environment. In banking, UPS systems can support servers, desktops, networks and ATM infrastructure. In IT and data-centre environments, reliable power is important for servers and other technology infrastructure. Manufacturing facilities may use UPS systems to protect CNC machines and precision equipment from power-related interruptions.

Healthcare facilities also depend on uninterrupted power for areas such as operation theatres, diagnostic equipment and other essential services. E&C's website also highlights UPS applications in real estate infrastructure and defence systems requiring clean and uninterrupted power.

Choose a UPS Manufacturer with Engineering Expertise

A UPS is an important part of a power protection system, particularly where interruptions or unstable power can affect equipment and operations. E&C combines its long-standing experience in power electronics with an extensive product portfolio covering Online UPS, Industrial Online UPS, Line Interactive UPS and other power solutions.

If you are evaluating a UPS manufacturer in India for commercial, industrial or critical power requirements, E&C provides a range of UPS systems designed for different capacities and applications.
