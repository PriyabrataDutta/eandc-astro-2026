Online UPS Manufacturer in India

Electronics & Controls Power Systems Pvt. Ltd. (E&C) is an established Online UPS Manufacturer in India, with more than 40 years of experience in power electronics. The company designs and manufactures Online UPS systems using modern technology and quality-focused engineering for applications where reliable and continuous power is essential.

With dedicated capabilities across research and development, manufacturing, sales and service, E&C provides Online UPS solutions for businesses and institutions across different sectors. Its applications include banking and finance, IT/ITES, manufacturing, healthcare, real estate and defence, where interruptions or unstable power can affect critical equipment and operations.

Reliable Online UPS Systems for Critical Power Requirements

An Online UPS provides continuous power protection by keeping connected equipment supplied with conditioned power. This makes Online UPS systems suitable for environments where even a brief power interruption or electrical disturbance can impact operations.

As an experienced Online UPS Manufacturer in India, E&C offers solutions across different capacities and configurations to address varying power requirements. Its product portfolio includes systems ranging from smaller-capacity UPS units to high-capacity solutions for larger installations.

Online UPS Product Range

E&C's Online UPS portfolio includes:

Accenta: 1 kVA to 10 kVA

Renata-I: 2 kVA to 30 kVA

Renata-300: 10 kVA to 800 kVA

Renata-DS: 10 kVA to 300 kVA

Renata Modular: 10 kVA to 1200 kVA

Accenta Rack: 1 kVA to 60 kVA

Lithium UPS: 1 kVA to 5 kVA

The company also manufactures Industrial Online UPS solutions, including MIL Grade UPS and IP41–IP55 Online UPS systems.

Why Choose E&C as an Online UPS Manufacturer in India?

Selecting an Online UPS manufacturer involves more than simply comparing capacity and specifications. Long-term reliability, engineering expertise, service support and the ability to address different application requirements are important considerations.

E&C has been active in the power electronics industry for more than four decades and has developed a broad portfolio of power products around its core UPS expertise. Its professional teams work across R&D, manufacturing, sales and service to support customers throughout the product lifecycle.

40+ Years of Power Electronics Experience

E&C brings more than four decades of experience in power electronics and Online UPS manufacturing. This long-standing engineering background supports the development of UPS systems for a wide range of commercial, industrial and institutional requirements.

Online UPS Solutions for Multiple Industries

E&C's Online UPS systems are used across several sectors where dependable power is important.

Banking & Finance: UPS systems support servers, desktops, networks, branches and ATM-related applications.

IT & ITES: Online UPS solutions are used for server racks, IT infrastructure and data-centre applications.

Manufacturing: Continuous and clean power can help protect CNC machines, precision tools and other sensitive manufacturing equipment from power-related interruptions.

Healthcare: Hospitals require dependable power for areas such as operation theatres, diagnostic equipment and other essential facilities.

Real Estate: UPS systems can support building monitoring systems, emergency lighting, elevators and other building infrastructure.

Defence: E&C states that its UPS systems are supplied for defence applications requiring clean and uninterrupted power, including equipment used by the Army, Air Force, Navy and BSF.

Online UPS Manufacturing and Engineering Capabilities

E&C combines product engineering with dedicated manufacturing and R&D capabilities. Its product portfolio extends beyond Online UPS systems to include Industrial Online UPS, Line Interactive UPS, industrial inverters, solar solutions, stabilizers, batteries, EV chargers, remote monitoring systems, transfer switches and other power products.

This broader product capability allows businesses to consider different power-management requirements through an experienced power-electronics company rather than treating UPS requirements as an isolated solution.
