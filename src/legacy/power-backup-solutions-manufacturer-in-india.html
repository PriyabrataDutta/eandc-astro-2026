Power Backup Solutions Manufacturer in India

Power Backup Solutions Manufacturer in India

Reliable power is essential for businesses and industries where even a short interruption can affect productivity, equipment, data and operations. As an established Power Backup Solutions Manufacturer in India, Electronics & Controls Power Systems Pvt. Ltd. (E&C) provides a wide range of power electronics solutions designed for different commercial, industrial and critical applications.

With more than four decades of experience in power electronics, E&C has developed expertise in designing and manufacturing Online UPS systems and other power backup solutions. The company combines engineering capabilities, manufacturing experience, research and development, sales and service support to deliver dependable power solutions for customers across India.

Comprehensive Power Backup Solutions for Different Requirements

Every organisation has different power protection requirements. A small office may require a compact UPS, while a manufacturing facility, healthcare institution or data centre may need a high-capacity Online UPS with reliable battery backup.

E&C offers multiple categories of power products, including Online UPS, Line Interactive UPS, domestic and industrial inverters, solar solutions, batteries, stabilizers, industrial battery chargers, remote monitoring solutions and other power accessories.

Online UPS Systems

E&C manufactures Online UPS systems across a broad capacity range. Its product portfolio includes Accenta, Renata-I, Renata-300, Renata-DS, Renata Modular, Accenta Rack and Lithium UPS systems.

These solutions are designed for applications where continuous and clean power is important, including IT infrastructure, healthcare, banking, manufacturing and other critical environments.

Industrial Power Backup Solutions

Industrial environments often require power systems capable of supporting demanding equipment and continuous operations. E&C provides Industrial Online UPS and Industrial Inverter solutions for applications where reliable power protection is essential.

Its industrial UPS portfolio includes specialised systems such as MIL Grade UPS and IP 41–55 Online UPS solutions.

Line Interactive UPS

For applications with comparatively less critical power requirements, E&C offers Line Interactive UPS systems. Its Cheerups range covers 600 VA to 5 kVA and includes features such as AVR-based voltage stabilisation, battery-mode efficiency and protection against power disturbances.

Batteries and Battery Charging Solutions

A dependable backup system also depends on the right battery technology. E&C's product range includes SMF, tubular, Ni-Cd, GEL and lithium-ion batteries. The company also manufactures industrial battery chargers for different power applications.

Power Backup Solutions for Multiple Industries

E&C serves customers across several sectors where dependable power is an important part of daily operations.

Banking and Financial Services

Banks, financial institutions, ATMs, servers, networks and other banking infrastructure require continuous power to maintain essential services. E&C's UPS systems are used for such applications.

IT and Data Infrastructure

IT companies and data-oriented facilities depend on clean and uninterrupted power for servers, racks, networks and other critical equipment. E&C provides UPS solutions for a range of IT and ITES applications.

Manufacturing

Power interruptions can affect CNC machines, precision tools, production schedules and manufacturing processes. E&C's power backup systems are designed to support manufacturing environments that require dependable power.

Healthcare

Hospitals depend on continuous electricity for operation theatres, diagnostic equipment, patient-care facilities and other essential systems. E&C supplies UPS solutions for healthcare applications where reliable backup power is required.

Real Estate and Infrastructure

Modern buildings use emergency lighting, elevators, building monitoring systems and other electrically powered infrastructure. E&C provides UPS and solar solutions for various real estate and building applications.

Defence and Critical Applications

Defence applications can require power systems capable of operating in demanding conditions. E&C's portfolio includes specialised UPS solutions designed for applications involving requirements such as shock and vibration resistance.

Power Backup Solutions Manufacturer in India with Engineering Expertise

Choosing a manufacturer rather than simply sourcing a power backup product can provide greater access to technical expertise, product knowledge and application-specific solutions.

E&C has more than 40 years of experience in power electronics and maintains capabilities across R&D, manufacturing, sales and service. Its portfolio also includes custom solutions, allowing power requirements to be addressed according to specific application needs.
