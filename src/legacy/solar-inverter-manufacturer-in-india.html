Solar Inverter Manufacturer in India

Electronics & Controls Power Systems Pvt. Ltd. (E&C) is an established solar inverter manufacturer in India, offering advanced power conversion solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional applications. With more than four decades of experience in power electronics, E&C combines engineering expertise with modern technology to develop reliable solar power solutions.

E&C's solar inverter portfolio includes Solar Grid-Tie Inverters and Solar Hybrid Inverters, providing different operating configurations for customers looking to integrate solar energy with grid power, batteries, and connected loads. The Solar Grid-Tie range is available from 2 kVA to 60 kVA, while the Solar Hybrid Inverter range covers 2 kVA to 30 kVA.

Solar Inverter Solutions for Different Power Requirements

Solar inverters play an important role in converting the DC electricity generated by solar panels into usable AC power. Depending on the system configuration, they can also help manage energy from the solar array, grid, and battery storage.

E&C provides solar inverter solutions designed around different energy requirements and operating conditions. Its products can support applications where solar energy is used for self-consumption, grid interaction, backup power, or a combination of these functions.

Solar Grid-Tie Inverter

E&C's Solar Grid-Tie Inverter is available in capacities from 2 kVA to 60 kVA. It uses a three-phase bridge transformerless topology and incorporates multiple MPPT channels to support efficient energy harvesting from solar arrays.

The inverter supports a wide DC input range of 200V to 800V and is designed with IP65 protection for indoor and outdoor environments. Its operating temperature range extends from -20°C to +60°C, making it suitable for a variety of installation conditions.

Solar Hybrid Inverter

The E&C Solar Hybrid Inverter is available from 2 kVA to 30 kVA and is designed to work with solar power, mains power, and batteries. It supports multiple operating modes, including off-grid, on-grid, and grid-tie with backup configurations.

The system features pure sine wave output, programmable supply priority for PV, battery, or grid power, adjustable charging current, and communication options such as USB, RS-232, Modbus, and SNMP.

Why Choose E&C as Your Solar Inverter Manufacturer in India?

Choosing the right solar inverter manufacturer is important because the inverter directly influences how effectively a solar power system manages and delivers generated energy. E&C brings extensive experience in power electronics and a broad portfolio of power solutions.

Over Four Decades of Power Electronics Experience

E&C has been operating in the power electronics sector since 1978, giving the company more than 40 years of experience in designing and manufacturing power solutions. Its product portfolio extends beyond solar solutions to include Online UPS systems, industrial inverters, stabilizers, EV chargers, batteries, and other power products.

Advanced Solar Inverter Technology

E&C's solar inverter solutions incorporate technologies such as MPPT, programmable operating modes, wide input voltage ranges, and communication interfaces. These features allow the systems to be configured according to different solar power requirements.

Solutions for Grid-Connected and Hybrid Applications

Different solar installations require different approaches to energy management. E&C offers both grid-tie and hybrid inverter solutions, allowing customers to select a system based on whether they need grid interaction, battery backup, solar self-consumption, or a combination of these functions.

Solar Inverters for Commercial and Industrial Applications

Solar energy is increasingly being integrated into commercial and industrial power systems to make better use of available renewable energy. E&C's solar inverter solutions can be considered for installations where dependable power conversion and energy management are important.

The appropriate inverter capacity depends on factors such as connected load, solar array capacity, operating configuration, battery requirements, and the desired relationship between solar, grid, and backup power.

E&C's wider customer base includes sectors such as banking and financial services, government, manufacturing, retail, and corporate organizations, reflecting its experience in providing power electronics solutions for different operating environments.
