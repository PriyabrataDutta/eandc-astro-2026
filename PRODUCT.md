## Platform

web

## Users
B2B buyers vetting a supplier: facility, electrical and procurement engineers at banks, hospitals, factories, data centres and government offices across India. They check that E&C is an established, capable manufacturer before asking for a quotation, usually comparing several suppliers.

## Product Purpose
The website of Electronics & Controls Power Systems Pvt. Ltd. (E&C Power), Bengaluru. It presents the company's UPS and power-electronics range and turns qualified visitors into enquiries and quote requests (form posts to /api/contact, or a call to sales). Success is a buyer who trusts E&C enough to request a quotation.

## Positioning
An Indian manufacturer since 1978 that designs, develops and builds its UPS and power electronics in-house at its own plant in Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru, with its own R&D and an all-India sales and service network (8 regional offices).

## Operating Context
Buyers arrive from search (city and product keywords), read product and category pages, compare capacities in kVA, and ask for a quotation or call sales. Pan-India SEO is a core goal: every page's title, description, canonical, H1 and JSON-LD are deliberate and must not change by accident.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Range: online UPS (1 to 1200 kVA), industrial UPS (MIL grade, IP41 to IP55, marine), line interactive UPS, inverters, voltage stabilizers, solar, EV chargers, batteries, battery chargers, remote monitoring, accessories, custom solutions (src/data/products.js).
- Site is Astro + Tailwind only; no legacy HTML, Bootstrap, jQuery or icon fonts.
- The live site www.eandcpower.com is in the client's own Vercel account; never touch it without client approval.

## Product Principles
- Prove capability with facts the client has stated, never with invented numbers or proof.
- Every page leads toward a quotation or a call with an engineer.
- Keep SEO signals exactly as published unless a change is intended and verified.
- Show E&C's own products, plant and people; stock imagery never stands in for them.
